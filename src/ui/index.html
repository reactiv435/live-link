    What does LIVE Link read?

    Only the number of taps (hearts) on your TikTok LIVE. It watches your LIVE the way a viewer does, so it never asks for your TikTok password and can't post, read your messages, or see your account.

    Those taps fill the hype bar for the song you're playing on reactivvibeai.com. Gifts and chat are not used yet.
