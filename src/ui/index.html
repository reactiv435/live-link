      What does LIVE Link read?

      The taps (hearts) and the gifts on your TikTok LIVE: for each gift, its name, its coin value, and the @handle and name of who sent it. It watches your LIVE the way a viewer does, so it never asks for your TikTok password and can't post, read your messages, or see your account.

      Taps and gifts fill the hype bar for the song you're playing on reactivvibeai.com (a gift is worth 10 taps per coin), and the overlay can thank the gifter by @handle. Chat is not used.
